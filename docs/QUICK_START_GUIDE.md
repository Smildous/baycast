# 🎯 Baycast — Quick Start Guide

Welcome to Baycast! This guide will get you from zero to your first forecast in under 5 minutes.

---

## 1. Create Your Account (3 Steps)

1. **Go to** [baycast-p.vercel.app](https://baycast-p.vercel.app)
2. **Sign up** with your email or social account (Google/GitHub)
3. **Pick a username** — this is what will appear on the leaderboard

✅ Done! You're in.

---

## 2. Make Your First Forecast (5 Steps)

### Step 1: Browse Questions
From the homepage, scroll through active questions organized by **blocks** (Politics, Tech, AI, Economics, Science). Click any question that interests you.

### Step 2: Read the Details
Each question includes:
- **The question** — clear, resolvable statement
- **Resolution criteria** — exactly how it will be judged
- **Deadline** — when forecasts lock
- **Background context** — relevant links and information

### Step 3: Make Your Prediction
Use the **probability slider** to enter your forecast:
- **0%** = definitely won't happen
- **50%** = coin flip
- **100%** = definitely will happen

⚠️ **Key rule:** You cannot see other people's predictions while making yours. This is the **Blind Consensus Protocol** — your judgment, uninfluenced.

### Step 4: Lock It In
Hit **"Submit Forecast"**. Once submitted, your prediction is locked. You cannot change it.

### Step 5: Wait for Resolution
When the deadline passes, the question closes. All forecasts are revealed simultaneously. Once the real-world outcome is determined, your accuracy is scored.

---

## 3. Understanding the Leaderboard

The leaderboard ranks forecasters by **accuracy**, not popularity.

| Column | What It Means |
|---|---|
| **Rank** | Your position based on cumulative accuracy score |
| **Brier Score** | Lower is better. Measures how close your probabilities were to outcomes. 0.0 = perfect. |
| **Questions** | Total number of questions you've forecasted on |
| **Streak** | Consecutive questions where you outperformed the crowd average |

### How Scoring Works
Baycast uses the **Brier score**, the gold standard in forecasting:
- Predict 90% on something that happens → great score ✅
- Predict 90% on something that doesn't happen → bad score ❌
- Predict 50% → neutral, no reward or penalty

The leaderboard updates in real-time as questions resolve.

---

## 4. How Questions Resolve

1. **Forecasting period** — Users submit predictions (blind to each other)
2. **Deadline** — No more submissions accepted
3. **Reveal** — All forecasts become visible
4. **Resolution** — The real-world outcome is determined by the question's resolution criteria
5. **Scoring** — Each forecast is scored and leaderboards update

### Who resolves questions?
Questions are resolved by Baycast admins based on the predefined resolution criteria. Criteria are always visible before you forecast, so you know exactly what you're predicting.

### How long does resolution take?
It depends on the question. Some resolve in days (e.g., "Will X happen this week?"), others in months (e.g., "Will X happen by December 2026?").

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Is Baycast gambling?
**No.** There is no money involved — no deposits, no wagers, no payouts. Baycast is a skill-based forecasting platform, similar to fantasy sports leagues or trivia competitions. You're playing for accuracy and reputation.

### Q2: What is the Blind Consensus Protocol?
It's our core mechanic. When you forecast, you cannot see what others have predicted. This eliminates herding behavior and anchoring bias — you rely purely on your own judgment. After the deadline, all forecasts are revealed together.

### Q3: How is this different from Polymarket or Metaculus?
Unlike Polymarket, there's no money trading — no regulatory gray zones. Unlike Metaculus, we focus on blind forecasting with gamified scoring and leaderboards. Baycast is designed to be fun, accessible, and free.

### Q4: Do I need crypto or a wallet?
**No.** Baycast requires no cryptocurrency, no wallet, and no blockchain interaction. Just sign up with email.

### Q5: Can I change my forecast after submitting?
**No.** Once submitted, forecasts are locked. This prevents gaming the system and makes the scoring meaningful.

### Q6: How do I improve my score?
- Forecast on questions within your expertise
- Practice calibration (if you say 70%, it should happen ~70% of the time)
- Read the resolution criteria carefully before predicting
- Avoid extreme probabilities unless you're very confident

### Q7: What are Question Blocks?
Blocks are themed categories (Politics, AI, Economics, etc.). Each block has its own leaderboard, so you can compete within your areas of knowledge.

### Q8: Is my data public?
Your username and forecasts are visible on public leaderboards after questions resolve. Your email and personal information are never shared.

### Q9: How often are new questions added?
New questions are added regularly. You can suggest questions through the platform's feedback channel.

### Q10: Is Baycast free?
**Yes, completely free.** No premium tiers, no paywalls, no hidden costs.
