## Tweet 4/7 — The Herding Problem

```
Prediction markets claim to aggregate "collective intelligence."

But research shows they suffer from the same herding behavior as stock markets:

3. High-volume events attract gamblers, not domain experts

When 80% of volume comes from people treating it like sports betting, you don't get wisdom.
```
