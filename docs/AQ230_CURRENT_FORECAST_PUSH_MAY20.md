## Stop rules

Stop sending if the app fails to load, sign-in breaks, or a forecast cannot be submitted.

Stop posting if a community moderator asks you to stop, removes the post, or says the ask is off-topic.

Stop using a question if its source looks broken, confusing, stale, or mismatched to the wording.

Stop adding context if you find yourself writing claims about traction, group views, public reads, or future features.

Stop the sequence after the first channel that produces real forecasts. Then spend time replying, fixing copy, and sending only targeted follow-ups.

Stop for the day if replies turn into debate about the product instead of forecasts. Capture the feedback, then move on.

## BCP-safe copy rules

Say "forecast before reading other forecasts" or "independent human judgment." Do not say people can see a group read.

Say "AI forecast writing is paused until the blind phase is safe." Do not promise AI activity today.

Say "source links are usable." Do not say source cleanup is still in progress.

Say "one forecast and one reason." Do not ask for likes, reposts, or pile-ons.

Do not include exact participation counts in outbound copy.

Do not claim traction, group direction, proof, accuracy, or a shared view.

Do not use finance words. Keep the frame as prediction polling and human judgment.

Do not invent names, testimonials, user behavior, or outcomes.

Do not mention internal queues, tickets, or implementation details beyond the safe AI pause line.

If unsure, send the shorter version. One clean ask is stronger than a clever explanation.
