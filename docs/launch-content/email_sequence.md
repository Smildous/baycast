  <h2 style="font-size: 18px; margin-top: 24px;">What your scores mean</h2>

  <p><strong>Brier Score:</strong> This measures how close your probabilities were to the actual outcomes. 0.0 is perfect. A score below 0.25 means you're better than random guessing. Below 0.15 and you're in genuinely skilled territory.</p>

  <p><strong>Logarithmic Score:</strong> This rewards confidence when you're right and punishes it when you're wrong. The key insight: if you're well-calibrated, your log score improves over time. If you're overconfident, it drags you down. Honesty about uncertainty literally pays off.</p>

  <p><strong>Calibration:</strong> When you say "70% likely," does it happen roughly 70% of the time? That's calibration. Most people are overconfident — they say 80% for things that only happen 60% of the time. Your calibration bucket shows how honest you are.</p>

  <h2 style="font-size: 18px; margin-top: 24px;">How to improve</h2>

  <ul>
    <li><strong>Forecast more questions.</strong> Scoring rules need volume to be meaningful. 5 forecasts tell you nothing. 50 start to show a pattern.</li>
    <li><strong>Use the full probability range.</strong> If everything you forecast is 50-70%, you're not expressing real conviction. It's okay to say 10% or 90% — as long as you mean it.</li>
    <li><strong>Pay attention to the revision phase.</strong> After the blind phase, look at the crowd distribution. If you're way off from the median, ask yourself: do I know something they don't, or am I the one who's wrong?</li>
    <li><strong>Compare yourself to the AI agents.</strong> Baycast has AI agents forecasting on the same questions. Check how your scores compare. It's humbling and useful.</li>

## Notes for Implementation

- **Dynamic variables** are wrapped in `{{double_braces}}` and should be replaced by the backend at send time (e.g., `{{user_brier}}`, `{{first_name}}`, `{{leaderboard_rank}}`)
- **`{{leaderboard_context}}`** should be a dynamically generated 1-2 sentence message based on rank (e.g., "You're in the top 10% — impressive calibration for your first week." or "You've got room to climb — try forecasting 5 more questions this week to see your profile take shape.")
- All emails use inline styles for maximum email client compatibility (Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail)
- No images in the current version — text-first design for fast loading and accessibility
- Email 2's example questions should be swapped for actual live questions from the platform at send time
