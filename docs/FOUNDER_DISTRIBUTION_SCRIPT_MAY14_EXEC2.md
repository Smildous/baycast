# Founder distribution script, May 14 exec2

If you only do one thing:
1. Publish 3 queued questions before posting anywhere.
2. Post the X block below with one live Baycast link.
3. Reply to your HN thread or update with the HN block below.
4. Send the 3 DMs to people who already care about forecasting, AI, or product signal.
5. Do not explain the whole product. Ask for one forecast and one criticism.

This is a 20 minute script for Smil to run today.

Current truth to say out loud if asked: Baycast has 1 user, 10 live questions, and 266+ queued questions. The bottleneck is not strategy or docs. The bottleneck is seed and publish. Publish enough good questions that a serious early user can land, answer, and feel the product in under two minutes.

Tone: honest, low hype, direct. No launch language. No claims that the market has spoken.

## Minute 0 to 3: publish before you distribute

Open the queue and publish 3 questions now.

Pick questions that meet all 3 rules:

1. A technical or market person can answer without research.
2. Resolution is clear enough that nobody has to trust your judgment.
3. The question is interesting even if Baycast is tiny.

Use this order:

First: one AI product or developer tooling question.
Second: one public company, market, or platform question.
Third: one Bay Area, startup, or internet culture question.

Do not polish the rest of the queue. Do not rewrite the docs. Do not add features. Publish 3, then distribute.

## Minute 3 to 6: X post

Post this from your personal account. Replace the bracketed link with the strongest live question URL.

```text
I am building Baycast, a free prediction polling site.

The honest state today: 1 user, 10 live questions, 266+ queued questions.

The useful part is blind consensus plus proper scoring. You forecast first, then see what other people think.

I need early users who will do one thing: answer 3 questions and tell me where the product feels wrong.

Start here: [LIVE QUESTION LINK]
```

If someone replies with interest, use this reply:

```text
Thank you. Best help is very small: answer a few questions before reading the crowd, then tell me which question felt ambiguous or boring. That is the signal I need right now.
```

If someone asks how it is different from gambling, use this reply:

```text
It is not gambling. No stakes, no trading, no odds market. It is free forecasting with scoring so people can compare calibration over time.
```

If someone says it is too early, use this reply:

```text
Yes, it is early. That is why I am asking for blunt usage, not praise. The product needs better seeded questions and a few serious forecasters before anything else matters.
```

## Minute 6 to 10: Hacker News comment or update

Use this only where it is natural: an existing Show HN thread, a relevant comment chain about forecasting, prediction markets, calibration, polling, AI evaluation, or collective intelligence. Do not spam a cold unrelated thread.

```text
I am the founder of Baycast. Quick honest update rather than a polished launch note:

Baycast currently has 1 user, 10 live questions, and 266+ queued questions. The bottleneck is me publishing and seeding good questions, not a lack of roadmap.

The core idea is simple: answer a question blind, then see the crowd. Over time, proper scoring should make calibration visible. It is free forecasting, not gambling, and there is no market or money layer.

If anyone here likes forecasting or calibration, I would value one specific kind of help: answer a few live questions and tell me which ones are unclear, uninteresting, or badly resolvable.

Link: [BAYCAST OR LIVE QUESTION LINK]
```

If someone gives a real criticism, reply with:

```text
That is useful. I agree the question quality has to carry the product, especially this early. I am going to publish fewer vague questions and bias toward ones with clean resolution criteria. Thank you for trying it.
```

If someone challenges the small user count, reply with:

```text
Fair. I am saying the small number because hiding it would be worse. Right now I am trying to learn whether the mechanic feels valuable to serious forecasters before pretending there is network value.
```

## Minute 10 to 14: Reddit follow-up

Use this as a follow-up comment only in a relevant existing post or in a community where you are allowed to share projects. Good fits: forecasting, prediction markets if they allow non-market tools, quantified self, startups, side projects, AI builders. Avoid communities where this reads as drive-by promotion.

```text

If this is relevant to the thread, I would appreciate one narrow thing: answer 3 questions and tell me which one should not have been published.

```

If a mod or user says it is promotional, reply once and stop:

```text
Understood. I will leave it there and not repost. I am looking for product feedback, but I get that it may not fit the thread.
```

If someone asks what feedback you want, reply:

```text
Mainly question quality. Was the resolution clear? Did you feel like you could answer without research? Did seeing the crowd after your forecast change anything?
```

## What not to do today

Do not write another positioning doc.
Do not describe Baycast as a prediction market.
Do not claim traction.
Do not ask for likes, reposts, or upvotes.
Do not post the same copy into unrelated communities.
Do not debate people who have not tried it.
Do not wait until all 266+ queued questions are cleaned up.

## Success condition for this 20 minute pass

A good outcome is not virality. A good outcome is 3 to 10 serious forecasts from people who might notice ambiguity, scoring issues, or weak question selection.

If that happens, publish more questions before doing more distribution.
