# SEO Content Ideas — Baycast Blog

**Prepared: May 2026**  
**Goal: Drive organic search traffic to Baycast via educational/informational content**

---

## Content Strategy Overview

Baycast's blog should target two types of search intent:
1. **Informational:** People searching for "how do prediction markets work," "what is the wisdom of crowds," etc.
2. **Comparison/Alternative:** People searching for "Polymarket alternatives," "prediction platforms without gambling," etc.

The blog also serves a secondary purpose: establishing Baycast as a thought leader in the forecasting space, which supports PR, Reddit credibility, and investor conversations.

---

## 10 Blog Post Ideas

### 1. "How Do Prediction Markets Work? A Complete Guide for Beginners"
- **Target keyword:** "how do prediction markets work" (est. 2,400 monthly searches)
- **Secondary keywords:** prediction market explained, binary options prediction, how to use prediction markets
- **Search volume rationale:** Core informational query with consistent volume. Every new Polymarket user searches this. High funnel — captures people new to the space.
- **Content angle:** Neutral, educational explainer. End with "if you're looking for a non-gambling alternative, check out Baycast."
- **Estimated word count:** 2,000-2,500

### 2. "Polymarket Alternatives: 8 Prediction Platforms Compared (2026)"
- **Target keyword:** "polymarket alternatives" (est. 1,800 monthly searches)
- **Secondary keywords:** sites like polymarket, prediction market apps, best prediction platforms 2026
- **Search volume rationale:** Growing query as Polymarket faces regulatory scrutiny and users look for alternatives. High commercial intent.
- **Content angle:** Honest comparison table. Include Polymarket, Metaculus, Manifold, Kalshi, PredictIt, Baycast, and 2-3 others. Highlight Baycast's blind consensus as unique differentiator.
- **Estimated word count:** 3,000-4,000

### 3. "What Is the Delphi Method? The Forecasting Technique That Outperformed Markets"
- **Target keyword:** "delphi method" (est. 3,600 monthly searches)
- **Secondary keywords:** delphi method forecasting, delphi technique explained, RAND corporation delphi study
- **Search volume rationale:** Academic/educational query with moderate volume. No dominant authority page — opportunity to own this topic.
- **Content angle:** Deep explainer of the Delphi method, its history, applications, and modern implementations (including Baycast's BCP).
- **Estimated word count:** 2,500-3,000

### 4. "Wisdom of Crowds vs. Herding: Why Seeing the Crowd Makes You Less Accurate"
- **Target keyword:** "wisdom of crowds herding" (est. 800 monthly searches, low competition)
- **Secondary keywords:** herding behavior prediction markets, anchoring bias forecasting, why crowds are wrong
- **Search volume rationale:** Niche academic query but highly relevant to Baycast's core thesis. Low competition means easier ranking.
- **Content angle:** Behavioral economics explainer with research citations. Include the 10-20% anchoring statistic and Good Judgment Project results.
- **Estimated word count:** 1,800-2,200

### 5. "What Is a Brier Score? How to Measure Forecasting Accuracy"
- **Target keyword:** "brier score" (est. 1,200 monthly searches)
- **Secondary keywords:** brier score explained, how to calculate brier score, forecasting accuracy metrics
- **Search volume rationale:** Technical query with dedicated searchers. No comprehensive, accessible explainer exists — Wikipedia is the top result.
- **Content angle:** Accessible math explainer with examples. Show how Brier scores work on Baycast. Include interactive calculator if possible.
- **Estimated word count:** 1,500-2,000

### 6. "The Good Judgment Project: How Amateur Forecasters Beat the CIA"
- **Target keyword:** "good judgment project" (est. 1,000 monthly searches)
- **Secondary keywords:** phil tetlock superforecasting, IARPA forecasting tournament, superforecasters book
- **Search volume rationale:** Growing interest in Tetlock's work. Ties directly to Baycast's methodology. Book-awareness searches.
- **Content angle:** Story-driven post about the Good Judgment Project, its methods, results, and relevance to modern forecasting platforms.
- **Estimated word count:** 2,000-2,500

### 7. "Prediction Markets Without Gambling: How Non-Money Platforms Compare"
- **Target keyword:** "prediction markets without gambling" (est. 500 monthly searches, growing)
- **Secondary keywords:** free prediction platforms, prediction polling vs prediction markets, non-gambling forecasting
- **Search volume rationale:** Emerging query driven by regulatory concerns and Google's ad restrictions. Low competition.
- **Content angle:** Compare Metaculus, Manifold, Baycast, and others. Highlight the regulatory and ethical advantages of non-gambling approaches.
- **Estimated word count:** 1,800-2,200

### 8. "Metaculus vs. Manifold vs. Polymarket: Which Prediction Platform Is Right for You?"
- **Target keyword:** "metaculus vs polymarket" (est. 600 monthly searches)
- **Secondary keywords:** manifold vs polymarket, best prediction platform, metaculus vs manifold
- **Search volume rationale:** Direct comparison queries indicate high purchase/registration intent. Multiple competitors = sustained demand.
- **Content angle:** Detailed comparison with use-case recommendations. Include Baycast as a fourth option with its unique positioning.
- **Estimated word count:** 2,500-3,000

### 9. "How AI Is Changing Forecasting: From Prediction Markets to Collective Intelligence"
- **Target keyword:** "AI prediction markets" (est. 900 monthly searches, growing rapidly)
- **Secondary keywords:** AI forecasting tools, artificial intelligence prediction accuracy, future of forecasting
- **Search volume rationale:** Explosive growth topic. AI + forecasting is a hot intersection (Metaculus's FutureEval, Atlantic/Vox coverage). Captures tech-forward audience.
- **Content angle:** Analysis of how AI tools are being used for forecasting, the role of human judgment, and where blind consensus fits in the AI era.
- **Estimated word count:** 2,000-2,500

### 10. "How to Become a Superforecaster: A Practical Guide to Improving Your Predictions"
- **Target keyword:** "how to become a superforecaster" (est. 400 monthly searches)
- **Secondary keywords:** superforecasting techniques, improve prediction accuracy, calibration training
- **Search volume rationale:** Long-tail but highly motivated audience. People searching this are likely to become active Baycast users.
- **Content angle:** Practical guide with techniques from the Good Judgment Project. Include Brier score tracking, calibration exercises, and platform recommendations (Baycast as the practice tool).
- **Estimated word count:** 2,500-3,000

## SEO Technical Notes

- **Target domain:** baycast.ai/blog (if custom domain acquired) or subdomain of vercel.app
- **Cross-posting:** Republish on Medium and/or dev.to for backlinks (with canonical URL pointing to Baycast blog)
- **Internal linking:** Link between blog posts (e.g., Brier Score post links from Polymarket Alternatives post)
- **Schema markup:** Implement Article schema for blog posts, FAQ schema for how-to posts
- **Image alt text:** Include target keywords in image descriptions
- **Update cadence:** Refresh comparison posts quarterly as competitor landscapes change
