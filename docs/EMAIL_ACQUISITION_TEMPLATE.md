## Email Body

**Hi {{first_name}},**

You've probably seen the headlines. Polymarket raised $400M at a $15B valuation. Congress is investigating insider trading. Romania banned 300 prediction market sites. The space is exploding — and imploding — at the same time.

Here's the thing nobody is talking about: **every prediction platform has the same structural flaw.** They all show you the crowd's answer before you predict. Polymarket shows you the price. Metaculus shows you the median. Manifold shows you the odds.

That's not forecasting. That's herding. Research shows it shifts your estimate by 10–20% toward the consensus — destroying the independent signal that makes crowds smart in the first place.

**We built something different.**

Baycast uses a **Blind Consensus Protocol** — you predict *before* seeing anyone else's answer. The aggregate stays hidden until everyone has committed. Then you can revise with full context. Your blind prediction is scored independently using proper Brier scoring.

It's based on the Delphi method (RAND Corporation, 1950s) and the Good Judgment Project (IARPA-funded, outperformed prediction markets by 25%). We just rebuilt it for the internet.

**No crypto. No real money. No gambling.** Just scored collective intelligence — a public good for understanding the future.

The proto is live with 10 questions. We're looking for **50 forecasters** to stress-test the protocol, build the first leaderboards, and shape the platform.

If you care about forecasting methodology — not gambling, not hype, not tokenomics — this is built for you.

## Sending Guidelines

- **Personalize the first line** whenever possible (name, community, specific prediction they made)
- **Send from a real email address** (smil@baycast.app or similar), not a noreply
- **Follow up once** after 5 days if no open/click
- **Keep the list small initially** — 50-200 highly-targeted contacts, not mass blast
- **Track metrics:** open rate, click-through to baycast-p.vercel.app, sign-up rate
- **Target open rate:** 40%+ (this audience opens emails from people they respect)
- **Target click-through:** 15%+ (the CTA is a single clear link)
