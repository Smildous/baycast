## 15 minute founder send sequence

0:00
Open the direct question links above. Pick the three that feel easiest for your audience. Do not send people into a maze if you already know what they care about.

2:00
Send the short DM to two people who will actually answer. Use the direct link that matches their world: AI builders get OpenAI or DeepMind, finance people get S&P or CPI, sports people get World Cup.

5:00
Post the X copy. Keep it plain. Do not add activity claims, public score claims, or anything that makes the product sound louder than it is.

7:00
Send the email to a tiny warm list. The ask is one forecast, not feedback on the whole product.

10:00
Post the HN or Discord note in one place where forecasting, AI evaluation, public reasoning, product feedback, or civic tech is already normal. If it feels off-topic, do not force it.

12:00
Reply personally to anyone who clicks, reacts, or asks what to do. Send them one direct question link and say: make your call before reading anyone else.

15:00
Stop broadcasting. Check whether the five human forecasts are happening. If not, send one more personal DM to the person most likely to finish the action right now.
