## Sending Guidelines

1. **Send 10–15 emails per day maximum** — don't burn through your list
2. **Personalize every email** — at minimum, the name and one specific reference to their work/community
3. **Send from a real email address** — smil@baycast.app or a professional personal address, not a generic contact@
4. **Track opens and replies** — use a simple spreadsheet (Date, Name, Community, Template, Sent, Opened, Replied, Follow-up 1, Follow-up 2, Result)
5. **Reply within 2 hours** of any response — speed signals genuine interest
6. **Never send mass emails** — if you can't personalize it, don't send it
7. **Bcc is for newsletters, not outreach** — always send individually
