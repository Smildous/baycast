## 3. First Comment Template (Maker Comment)

> **Post this as the FIRST comment immediately after launching.**

---

**Hey Product Hunt! 👋**

We're the team behind **Baycast**, and we're building the future of collective forecasting — without the gambling.

**The story:** We got frustrated watching prediction markets become crypto casinos. Polymarket, Kalshi, Metaculus — they all have merit, but they've drifted toward trading, speculation, and regulatory gray zones. We asked a simple question: *what if you could harness the wisdom of crowds without any of that baggage?*

That's how **Baycast** was born. Here's what makes us different:

### 🔒 Blind Consensus Protocol (BCP)
The core innovation. When you make a forecast, you **cannot see what others predicted**. This eliminates anchoring bias, herding, and groupthink. You predict purely on your own judgment. After the deadline, all forecasts are revealed simultaneously — and that's when the magic happens.

### 📊 Accuracy Scoring
Every forecast is scored using a proper scoring rule (Brier score). Over time, you build a track record. We surface the most accurate forecasters on our leaderboard — not the loudest or the most popular, but the *best*.

### 🧱 Question Blocks
Questions are organized into curated blocks — politics, tech, AI, economics, science. Each block has its own leaderboard, making it easy to find your domain and compete with peers.

**Why now?** Prediction markets are under increasing regulatory scrutiny. AI forecasting is booming (Metaculus, Hypermind, AI tournaments). Collective intelligence is having a moment. We think there's room for a **free, accessible, no-gambling** platform that anyone can use.

We're still early — this is our prototype. But the core mechanics work, and we'd love your feedback. Try making your first forecast and tell us what you think!

→ **[Try Baycast](https://baycast-p.vercel.app)**
