## 1. Will Google announce an AI feature for Google Search that is explicitly limited to paid subscribers during Google I/O 2026?

- title: Will Google announce an AI feature for Google Search that is explicitly limited to paid subscribers during Google I/O 2026?
- category: tech
- closes_at: 2026-05-22T23:59:59Z
- resolution source: Google official blog, Google I/O keynote replay, or The Keyword recap for Google I/O 2026.
- criteria: Resolves YES if Google publicly announces during Google I/O 2026 a new or expanded AI feature inside Google Search and states that access is limited to a paid plan, subscription, or paid tier at launch. Resolves NO if no such Search feature is announced by the close time.

- estimated base probability: 38%
