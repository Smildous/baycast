Title: Baycast is live early. Looking for 10 real forecasts tonight.

I am building Baycast, a prediction polling protocol for forecasting and collective intelligence.

Current state is small and plain: 44 live questions, 2 users, 1 forecast.

The main mechanic is Blind Consensus. You make your forecast before seeing the crowd view. The point is to protect the independent first read, then compare it with the group.

Tonight I am trying to get 10 human forecasts, not a launch spike. If you have a minute, pick any open question, add one forecast, and tell me what felt confusing, slow, or unnecessary.
