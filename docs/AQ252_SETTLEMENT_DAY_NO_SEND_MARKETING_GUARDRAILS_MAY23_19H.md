## 10-minute pre-outbound checklist

Set a timer for ten minutes. If the timer ends with any doubt, do not send.

Minute 0 to 2: open the settled question in a clean signed-out browser. Confirm the URL is public, not an admin route, not staging, not a private preview.

Minute 2 to 4: verify the page shows the original question, the final outcome, the public settlement note, and the visible score. The score must be on the page, not inferred from logs or a private dashboard.

Minute 4 to 5: compare the settlement note with the outcome source. The note should be readable by a stranger and should not need private context.

Minute 5 to 6: check the copy. It must say prediction polling, forecast, outcome, settlement, or score. It must not say bet, betting, wager, gambling, odds, payout, stake, casino, alpha, lock, or moon.

Minute 6 to 7: check the claim size. The only safe claim is that one public Baycast question has settled and the score is visible. No users claim. No growth claim. No “people are sharing it”. No “validated”.

Minute 7 to 8: check the channel. There must be an approved authenticated sender account. Do not use scripts, cron, a borrowed account, a fresh account, or this VPS as a sending machine.

Minute 8 to 9: check the recipients. For DMs, there must be a real private recipient list with a reason for each person. If the list is guessed, scraped, bought, generated, or missing, nothing goes out.

Minute 9 to 10: do one last signed-out reload of the link. If the settlement note and score are still visible, save the link and screenshot for the sender. If anything breaks, write “blocked, no send” in the operating note.
