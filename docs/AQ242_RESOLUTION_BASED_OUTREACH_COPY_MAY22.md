## HN comment-style explanation

Baycast is a prediction polling project. The main product idea is not the question list, it is the full loop after a question resolves.

A user makes a forecast before seeing the crowd view. The question later settles against public criteria. Then the page shows the outcome, the settlement note, and the score state. That makes it possible to judge calibration after the fact instead of only reacting to opinions while the question is open.

The first settled page is here:

https://baycast-p.vercel.app/questions/13aa9f2f-3226-4213-a04f-0cc2b87ad248

I am trying to keep the language close to public polling and forecasting. The useful feedback is whether the settlement note is clear, whether the scoring is legible, and whether the page gives enough evidence for someone who did not follow the question while it was open.

## Founder note

Baycast is built around a simple belief: forecasts get more useful when the outcome is public and the score is visible.

The first settlement is the moment the product starts to show that loop. Before that, Baycast is mostly a list of open questions. After settlement, there is a record: what was asked, what counted as resolution, what happened, and how forecasts were scored.

That is why this outreach waits until after the settlement note is live. The right ask is not "come join a new app." The right ask is: does this resolved page make the forecast, outcome, and score easy to understand?
