A 20%+ upward adjustment is rare but possible with a large hashrate increase (e.g., major mining operations coming online, post-halving efficiency improvements).     - End date: 2026-09-30

    - Source: https://btc.com/stats/difficulty; https://www.coinwarz.com/mining/bitcoin/difficulty-chart
