How do prediction markets work?

It's simple: contracts are priced between $0 and $1.

If "Will the Fed cut rates by July?" trades at $0.35, the market says there's a 35% chance.

Buy Yes if you think it's higher. Buy No if you think it's lower. Price = probability.
